About us

לחם בהר

Hello everyone.

We are לחם בהר — a home boutique bakery specializing in fresh sourdough breads and special bakes.

What we bake

  • A range of distinctive sourdough loaves
  • Sourdough challah without commercial yeast
  • Crisp whole-spelt crackers, and a gluten-free option
  • Frozen sourdough pizza bases
  • A giant brown-butter chocolate chip cookie with dark chocolate and Maldon salt

Ordering & delivery

Orders are open through Wednesday at 20:00. Deliveries go out on Friday.

We deliver to: Ma’ale HaHamisha, Kiryat Anavim, Beit Nekofa, Neve Ilan, Yad HaShmona, Sho’eva, Shoresh, Har Adar, and Mevaseret Zion.

Pickup points: Neve Ilan (Etz HaRimon 75), Kiryat Anavim, Yehuda Winery in Moshav Shoresh, and Mevaseret Zion (HaRotem 15).

We cannot promise an exact arrival time — we bake as close as possible to your order — and delivery is free of charge.
